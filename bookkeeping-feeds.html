Feeds

Every raw line that has entered the system, as its statement, check pack or supplier invoice reported it. A bookkeeping project claims them by date window, and only then do they become transactions. Bank and check values are correctable by an admin, which discards whatever was built on them and lets the pipeline rebuild it.

Unclaimed — in no project’s window, or its window is not yet open Pending — claimed, not yet routed Routed — became a project transaction Matched — check paired to a bank line
