Feeds

Every raw line that has entered the system, exactly as its statement, check pack or supplier invoice reported it. Feeds are immutable — a bookkeeping project claims them by date window, and only then do they become transactions.

Unclaimed — in no project’s window, or its window is not yet open Pending — claimed, not yet routed Routed — became a project transaction Matched — check paired to a bank line
